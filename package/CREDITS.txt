Dynamic Campaign Japanese Localization

Original Dynamic Campaign and English campaign text: Bungalow

Plugin loading framework: Anchor Chain, SeaPower-Modders
https://github.com/SeaPower-Modders/AnchorChain

https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony

Japanese text was translated and reviewed against the original English.
The Japanese localization DLL is independently implemented in this project.
Game and framework DLLs are referenced from the user's installed environment and are not included in this package.
